star

star – (*) multiplication operator

Description

Multiplication. Usual meaning. Valid for constant, boolean, polynomial, rational matrices and for syslin lists (the meaning is series connection) Element-wise multiplication is denoted x.*y. If x or y is scalar (1x1 matrix)  .*  is the same as *. Kronecker product is x.*.y

See also mulf syslin
