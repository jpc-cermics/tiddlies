pvm_exit

pvm_exit – tells the local pvmd that this process is leaving PVM.

Calling sequence

  [info] = pvm_exit()

Parameters

  • info: integer

Description

pvm_exit tells the local pvmd that this process is leaving PVM. This routine does not kill the process, which can continue to perform tasks just like any other serial process.pvm_exit should be called by all PVM processes before they stop or exit for good. It must be called by processes that were not started with pvm_spawn.
