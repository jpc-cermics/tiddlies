bstap

bstap – hankel approximant

Calling sequence

  [Q]=bstap(Sl)

Parameters

  • sl: linear system (syslin list) assumed continuous-time and anti-stable.
  • Q: best stable approximation of Sl (syslin list).

Description

Computes the best approximant Q of the linear system Sl where ——T—— is the H-infinity norm of the Hankel operator associated with Sl.

See also syslin
