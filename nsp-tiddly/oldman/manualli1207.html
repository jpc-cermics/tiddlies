beep

beep – Produce a beep sound

Calling sequence

  beep();  
  beep('on')  
  beep('off')  
  s=beep()

Description

beep() produces your computer's default beep sound.beep('on') turns the beep onbeep('off') turns the beep offs=beep() returns the current beep mode (on or off).
