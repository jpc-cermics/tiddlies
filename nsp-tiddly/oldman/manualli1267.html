convstr

convstr – Not implemented in nsp

Description

Not implemented in nsp. You can use instead toupper, or tolower, or capitalize.

Examples

  A=['You','must','believe','in','spring']
  toupper(A)
  tolower(A)
  capitalize(A)

See also toupper tolower capitalize
