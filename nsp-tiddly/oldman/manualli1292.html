recur

recur – a bilinear recurrent equation

Calling sequence

  [y]=recur(x0,var,k,n)  
  [integr]=logr(k,var)

Description

computes solutions of a bilinear recurrent equation

  x(i+1)=-x(i)*(k + sqrt(var)*br(i))

with initial value x0 and driven by a white noise of variance var. Trajectories are drawn and the empirical Lyapunov exponent is returned ( x(i) is not to much different from exp(y*i) ) A theoretical computation of the Lyapunov exponent is given by

  [integr]=logr(k,var)
