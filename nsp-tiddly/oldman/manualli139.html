coth

coth – hyperbolic cotangent

Calling sequence

  [t]=coth(x)

Description

the elements of vector t are the hyperbolic cotangent of elements of the vector x.

Examples

  x=[1,2*%i]
  t=exp(x);
  (t-ones(x) ./t) .\(t+ones(x) ./t)
  coth(x)

See also cotg
