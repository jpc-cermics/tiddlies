cumsum

cumsum – cumulative sum

Calling sequence

  y=cumsum(x)  
  y=cumsum(x,'r') or y=cumsum(x,1)  
  y=cumsum(x,'c') or y=cumsum(x,2)

Parameters

  • x: vector or matrix (real or complex)
  • y: vector or matrix (real or complex)

Description

For a vector or a matrix x, y=cumsum(x) returns in y the cumulative sum of all the entries of x taken columnwise.y=cumsum(x,'c') (or, equivalently, y=cumsum(x,2)) returns in y the cumulative sum of the columns of x:  y(i,:)=cumsum(x(i,:))y=cumsum(x,'r') (or, equivalently, y=cumsum(x,1)) returns in y the cumulative sum of the rows of x:  y(:,i)=cumsum(x(:,i))

Examples

  A=[1,2;3,4];
  cumsum(A)
  cumsum(A,'r')
  cumsum(A,'c')
  a=rand(3,4)+%i;
  [m,n]=size(a);
  w=zeros(a);
  w(1,:)=a(1,:);
  for k=2:m do w(k,:)=w(k-1,:)+a(k,:);end;w-cumsum(a,'r')
