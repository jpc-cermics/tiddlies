minus

minus – (-) substraction operator, sign changes

Calling sequence

  X-Y  
  -Y

Parameters

  • X: scalar or vector or matrix of numbers, polynomials or rationals. It may also be a syslin list
  • Y: scalar or vector or matrix of numbers, polynomials or rationals. It may also be a syslin list

Description

Substraction For numeric operands substraction as its usual meaning. If one of the operands is a matrix and the other one a scalar the the operation is performed element-wise. if Y==[] X is returned; if X==[] -Y is returned. Substraction may also be defined for other data types through ”soft-coded” operations.

Examples

  [1,2]-1
  []-2
  %s-2
  1/%s-2
  "cat"+"enate"

See also addf mtlb_mode
