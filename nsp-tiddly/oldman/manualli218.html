pertrans

pertrans – pertranspose

Calling sequence

  [Y]=pertrans(X)

Parameters

  • X: real or complex matrix
  • Y: real or complex matrix

Description

Y=pertrans(X) returns the pertranspose of X, i.e. the symmetric of X w.r.t the second diagonal (utility function).

Examples

  A=[1,2;3,4]
  pertrans(A)
