prod

prod – product

Calling sequence

  y=prod(x)  
  y=prod(x,'r') or y=prod(x,1)  
  y=prod(x,'c') or y=prod(x,2)

Parameters

  • x: real or complex vector or matrix
  • y: real or complex scalar or matrix

Description

For a vector or a matrix x, y=prod(x) returns in the scalar y the prod of all the entries of x, , e.g. prod(1:n) is n!y=prod(x,'r') (or, equivalently, y=prod(x,1))computes the rows elementwise product of x. y is the row vector: y(i)=prod(x(i,:)).y=prod(x,'c') (or, equivalently, y=prod(x,2)) computes the columns elementwise product of x. y is the column vector: y(i)=prod(x(:,i)).prod is not implemented for sparse matrices.

Examples

  A=[1,2;0,100];
  prod(A)
  prod(A,'c')
  prod(A,'r')

See also sum cumprod
