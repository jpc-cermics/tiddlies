sinh

sinh – hyperbolic sine

Calling sequence

  [t]=sinh(x)

Parameters

  • x,t: real or complex vectors/matrices

Description

the elements of vector t are the hyperbolic sine of elements of vector x.

Examples

  asinh(sinh([0,1,%i]))
