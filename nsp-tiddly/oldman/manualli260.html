tanh

tanh – hyperbolic tangent

Calling sequence

  t=tanh(x)

Description

the elements of t are the hyperbolic tangents of the elements of x

Examples

  x=[1,%i,-1,-%i]
  tanh(x)
  sinh(x) ./cosh(x)

See also atanh tan tanhm
