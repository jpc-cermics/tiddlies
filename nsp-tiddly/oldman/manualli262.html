tan

tan – tangent

Calling sequence

  [t]=tan(x)

Parameters

  • x: vector or matrix
  • t: vector or matrix

Description

The elements of t are the tangent of the elements of x.

Examples

  x=[1,%i,-1,-%i]
  tan(x)
  sin(x) ./cos(x)

See also atan tanm
