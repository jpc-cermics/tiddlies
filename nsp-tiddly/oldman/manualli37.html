equil

equil – balancing of pair of symmetric matrices

Calling sequence

  T=equil(P,Q)

Parameters

  • P, Q: two positive definite symmetric matrices
  • T: nonsingular matrix

Description

equil returns t such that:T*P*T' and inv(T)'*Q*inv(T) are both equal to a same diagonal and positive matrix.

Examples

  P=rand(4,4);P=P*P';
  Q=rand(4,4);Q=Q*Q';
  T=equil(P,Q)
  clean(T*P*T')
  clean(inv(T)'*Q*inv(T))
