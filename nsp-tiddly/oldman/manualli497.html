secto3d

secto3d – 3D surfaces conversion

Calling sequence

  [m[,x]]=secto3d(seclist,npas)  
  [m]=secto3d(seclist ,x)

Parameters

  • seclist: a list whose elements are (2,.) matrices
  • npas: an integer
  • m: a matrix
  • x: a vector

Description

Considering a surface given through a list seclist of sections in the (x,z) plane [m [,x]]=secto3d(seclist [,npas]) returns a matrix m which contains a regular discretization of the surface.

  • - The i-th row of the matrix m corresponds to the i-th section
  • - The j-th column of m corresponds to the x(j)

Each section seclist(i) is described by a (2,.) matrix which gives respectively the x and z coordinates of points.[m]=secto3d(seclist ,x): in that case the x-vector gives the discretization of the x-axis for all the sections

See also plot3d
