fullrfk

fullrfk – full rank factorization of Ak

Calling sequence

  [Bk,Ck]=fullrfk(A,k)

Parameters

  • A: real or complex matrix
  • k: integer
  • Bk,Ck: real or complex matrices

Description

This function computes the full rank factorization of A^k i.e. Bk*Ck=A^k where Bk is full column rank and Ck full row rank. One has range(Bk)=range(A^k) and ker(Ck)=ker(A^k). For k=1, fullrfk is equivalent to fullrf.

Examples

  A=rand(5,2)*rand(2,5);[Bk,Ck]=fullrfk(A,3);
  norm(Bk*Ck-A^3,1)

See also fullrf range
