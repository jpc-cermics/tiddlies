givens

givens – Givens transformation

Calling sequence

  U=givens(xy)  
  U=givens(x,y)  
  [U,c]=givens(xy)  
  [U,c]=givens(x,y)

Parameters

  • x,y: two real or complex numbers
  • xy: real or complex size 2 column vector
  • U: 2x2 unitary matrix
  • c: real or complex size 2 column vector

Description

U = givens(x, y) or U = givens(xy) with xy = [x;y] returns a 2x2 unitary matrix U such that:U*xy=[r;0]=c. Note that givens(x,y) and givens([x;y]) are equivalent.

Examples

  A=[3,4;5,6];
  U=givens(A(:,1));
  U*A
