orth

orth – orthogonal basis

Calling sequence

  Q=orth(A)

Parameters

  • A: real or complex matrix
  • Q: real or complex matrix

Description

Q=orth(A) returns Q, an orthogonal basis for the span of A. Range(Q) = Range(A) and Q'*Q=eye. The number of columns of Q is the rank of A as determined by the QR algorithm.

Examples

  A=rand(5,3)*rand(3,4);
  [X,dim]=rowcomp(A);X=X';
  svd([orth(A),X(:,1:dim)])

See also qr rowcomp colcomp range
