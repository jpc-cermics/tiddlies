projspec

projspec – spectral operators

Calling sequence

  [S,P,D,i]=projspec(A)

Parameters

  • A: square matrix
  • S, P, D: square matrices
  • i: integer (index of the zero eigenvalue of A).

Description

Spectral characteristics of A at 0.S = reduced resolvent at 0 (S = -Drazin_inverse(A)).P = spectral projection at 0.D = nilpotent operator at 0.index = index of the 0 eigenvalue. One has (s*eye()-A)^(-1) = D^(i-1)/s^i +... + D/s^2 + P/s - S - s*S^2 -... around the singularity s=0.

Examples

  deff('j=jdrn(n)','j=zeros(n,n);for k=1:n-1;j(k,k+1)=1;end')
  A=sysdiag(jdrn(3),jdrn(2),rand(2,2));X=rand(7,7);
  A=X*A*inv(X);
  [S,P,D,index]=projspec(A);
  index,//size of J-block
  trace(P),//sum of dimensions of J-blocks
  A*S-(eye()-P)
  norm(D^index,1)
