trace

trace – trace

Calling sequence

  trace(X)

Parameters

  • X: real or complex square matrix, polynomial or rational matrix.

Description

trace(X) is the trace of the matrix X. Same as sum(diag(X)).

Examples

  A=rand(3,3);
  trace(A)-sum(spec(A))

See also det
