arc_graph

arc_graph – graph with nodes corresponding to arcs

Calling sequence

  g1 = arc_graph(g)

Parameters

  • g: graph list of the old graph
  • g1: graph list of the new graph

Description

arc_graph returns the directed graph g1 with the nodes corresponding to the arcs of the directed graph g. g1 is defined in the following way: - its nodes correspond to the arcs of g - 2 nodes of the new graph are adjacent if and only if the corresponding arcs of the graph g are consecutive. The coordinates of the nodes of g1 are given by the middle points of the corresponding edges of g. If such an arc graph does not exist, an empty vector is returned.

Examples

  ta=[1,1,2,4,4,5,6,7,2,3,5,1];
  he=[2,6,3,6,7,8,8,8,4,7,3,5];
  g=make_graph('foo',1,8,ta,he);
  g('node_x')=[281,284,360,185,405,182,118,45];
  g('node_y')=[262,179,130,154,368,248,64,309];
  show_graph(g);
  g1=arc_graph(g);
  show_graph(g1,'new');
