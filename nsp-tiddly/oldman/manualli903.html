derivat

derivat – rational matrix derivative

Calling sequence

  pd=derivat(p)

Parameters

  • p: polynomial or rational matrix

Description

computes the derivative of the polynomial or rational function matrix w.r.t the dummy variable.

Examples

  s=poly(0,'s');
  derivat(1/s),// -1/s^2;
