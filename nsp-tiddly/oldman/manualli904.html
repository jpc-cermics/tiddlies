determ

determ – determinant of polynomial matrix

Calling sequence

  res=determ(W [,k])

Parameters

  • W: real square polynomial matrix
  • k: integer (upper bound for the degree of the determinant of W)

Description

returns the determinant of a real polynomial matrix (computation made by FFT).res=determ(W [,k])k is an integer larger than the actual degree of the determinant of W. The default value of k is the smallest power of 2 which is larger than n*maxi(degree(W)). Method: evaluate the determinant of W for the Fourier frequencies and apply inverse FFT to the coefficients of the determinant.

Examples

  s=poly(0,'s');
  w=s*rand(10,10);
  determ(w)
  det(coeff(w,1))*s^10

See also det detr coffg
