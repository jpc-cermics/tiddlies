hrmt

hrmt – gcd of polynomials

Calling sequence

  [pg,U]=hrmt(v)

Parameters

  • v: row of polynomials i.e. 1xk polynomial matrix
  • pg: polynomial
  • U: unimodular matrix polynomial

Description

[pg,U]=hrmt(v) returns a unimodular matrix U and pg = gcd of row of polynomials v such that v*U = [pg,0].

Examples

  x=poly(0,'x');
  v=[x*(x+1),x^2*(x+1),(x-2)*(x+1),(3*x^2+2)*(x+1)];
  [pg,U]=hrmt(v);U=clean(U)
  det(U)

See also gcd htrianr
