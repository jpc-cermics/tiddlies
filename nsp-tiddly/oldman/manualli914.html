lcmdiag

lcmdiag – least common multiple diagonal factorization

Calling sequence

  [N,D]=lcmdiag(H)  
  [N,D]=lcmdiag(H,flag)

Parameters

  • H: rational matrix
  • N: polynomial matrix
  • D: diagonal polynomial matrix
  • flag: character string: 'row' or 'col' (default)

Description

[N,D]=lcmdiag(H,'row') computes a factorization D*H=N, i.e. H=D^(-1)*N where D is a diagonal matrix with D(k,k)=lcm of kth row of H('den').[N,D]=lcmdiag(H) or [N,D]=lcmdiag(H,'col) returns H=N*D^(-1) with diagonal D and D(k,k)=lcm of kth col of H('den')

Examples

  s=poly(0,'s');
  H=[1/s,(s+2)/s/(s+1)^2;1/(s^2*(s+2)),2/(s+2)];
  [N,D]=lcmdiag(H);
  N/D-H
