ldiv

ldiv – polynomial matrix long division

Calling sequence

  [x]=ldiv(n,d,k)

Parameters

  • n,d: two real polynomial matrices
  • k: integer

Description

x=ldiv(n,d,k) gives the k first coefficients of the long division of n by d i.e. the Taylor expansion of the rational matrix [nij(z)/dij(z)] near infinity. Coefficients of expansion of nij/dij are stored in x((i-1)*n+k,j)  k=1:n

Examples

  wss=ssrand(1,1,3);[a,b,c,d]=abcd(wss);
  wtf=ss2tf(wss);
  x1=ldiv(numer(wtf),denom(wtf),5)
  x2=[c*b;c*a*b;c*a^2*b;c*a^3*b;c*a^4*b]
  wssbis=markp2ss(x1',5,1,1);
  wtfbis=clean(ss2tf(wssbis))
  x3=ldiv(numer(wtfbis),denom(wtfbis),5)

See also arl2 markp2ss pdiv
